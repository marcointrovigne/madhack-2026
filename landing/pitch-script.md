# Pitch script · ~3 minutes

5 slides · target 30–45 sec per slide.
**Bold** = land the line. *(italic)* = stage direction.

---

## SLIDE 01 · TITLE   (~15s)

> "Hi, I'm Marco. We're building **Treasury AI** — Mercury Treasury for Europe.
>
> An AI treasurer that earns **4%+ on idle startup cash, automatically.**
>
> Built this weekend on Alpaca, Orca, LangGraph and Claude. Let me show you the gap we close."

*(advance →)*

---

## SLIDE 02 · THE GAP   (~40s)

> "Every small European company — startup, SMB, family business — has anywhere from five hundred thousand to ten million euros sitting in a checking account at 0.5%.
>
> T-bills currently pay over 4%. **The gap on a typical €3M is one hundred and five thousand euros — every single year.**
>
> Why doesn't anyone capture it? *(beat — gesture at the four reasons)*
>
> Opening a brokerage is weeks of paperwork. Writing an investment policy is a board-level conversation. Forecasting cashflow needs a CFO they can't afford. Monthly rebalancing is brain damage.
>
> So the cash sits idle, inflation eats it, and the founder feels vague guilt.
>
> **Across Europe, that's one and a half trillion euros earning under one percent.**"

*(advance →)*

---

## SLIDE 03 · THE PRODUCT   (~45s)

> "We fix it with a **multi-agent AI treasurer**. The customer chats with one agent. Six specialists collaborate behind the scenes — each one mirrors a real corporate treasury function.
>
> *(point at the diagram)*
>
> The **Forecaster** reads three years of bank transactions and projects cashflow. The **Strategist** builds a T-bill ladder using live yields from Alpaca. The **Risk Officer** enforces the investment policy and has hard veto power. The **Executor** places trades. And the **CFO** turns all of that into a single paragraph the founder can read.
>
> **Critically: every trade requires explicit approval.** Full audit trail. The AI proposes — the founder decides."

*(advance → demo)*

---

## SLIDE 04 · LIVE DEMO   (~60s)

> "Here's what that actually looks like. *(walk down the terminal)*
>
> Founder asks: *show me where my cash is.* — Portfolio agent returns balance.
>
> *Burn rate and deployable cash?* — Forecaster runs cashflow projection on three years of bank data.
>
> *We just got fifty thousand extra. What now?* — Strategist builds a ladder.
>
> **And here's the wow moment.** *(point at the red VETO line)*
>
> The Risk Officer flags an over-concentration violation. The Strategist revises. The CFO comes back with a clean proposal.
>
> Founder types *approve.* The Executor places four real paper trades on Alpaca. Receipts come back.
>
> **It looks like a real institution debating internally — because it is one.**"

*(advance →)*

*(open Q&A)*

## One-liner fallbacks

- **5-second elevator:** "AI treasurer for European startups and SMBs — 4% on idle cash, automatically."
- **If asked about safety:** "Money is custodied at Alpaca. We're a software layer. Same model as Wealthfront and Mercury."
- **If asked about regulation:** "Investment-advisor model — same as Indexa, Moneyfarm, Scalable."
- **If asked why now:** "Rates went from zero to five. Suddenly idle cash has a real cost. And LLMs make a chat-first treasurer finally possible."
- **If asked "T-bills or ETFs?":** "T-bill ETFs — SGOV, BIL, SHV, SHY. They hold nothing but US Treasury bills. Same yield, same safety, but tradeable through Alpaca's API which is what makes the automation possible. Expense ratio ~10 bps, included in our quoted yields."
