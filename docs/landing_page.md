## SECTION 10: FAQ

#### Is this legal in Europe?

Yes. We operate as an investment advisor — the same regulatory model as Indexa Capital, Moneyfarm, Scalable, Wealthfront, Betterment. Customer money is custodied at a regulated broker (Alpaca).

#### What if interest rates drop?

Our agent rebalances monthly. If rates drop, it shortens duration. If they rise, it extends. You always have current best yield.

#### What if I need cash back urgently?

Half the portfolio is in 0–3 month T-bills (T+1 settlement). Cash back in your bank within 48 hours. For genuine emergencies, we can liquidate everything within a week.

#### Can I see every decision the AI makes?

Yes. Every proposal includes the reasoning. Every trade has an audit trail. You can sidebar any agent (Risk Officer, Forecaster, Strategist) to interrogate its logic directly.

#### What if my investment policy is unusual?

You can customize it: max % per ETF, max duration, allowed asset classes, minimum credit rating, liquidity floor. The Risk Officer enforces *your* rules.

#### How is this different from Mercury?

Mercury is US-only. We're EU-native. Their fee structure is opaque (a spread on the yield curve); ours is transparent (20 bps flat). We're also AI-agent-native — chat instead of dashboards.
